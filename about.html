about us

our story

Rise Coffee began as a pop-up espresso cart in 2016, run by Jenny & Mark who believed mornings deserved better. What started with a borrowed machine and a handful of regulars has grown into a community hub where great coffee meets genuine connection. We’ve stayed true to our roots: small-batch roasting, a love for craft, and a commitment to keeping coffee approachable.
